      Welcome

      Set up Synapse in about two minutes

      Synapse listens to your interview through the speakers or headphones you already use, and drafts answers from your own resume as each question ends.

      You never need to change your Mac's sound output or install audio drivers.

      Step 1 of 5 · Permissions

      Let Synapse hear your call

      macOS asks once for permission to record system audio. Synapse only listens while Listen is on, and macOS shows its recording indicator in the menu bar whenever it does.

      MicrophoneOptional. Lets Synapse transcribe your own answers.

      Step 2 of 5 · Audio test

      Check that Synapse can hear your Mac

      Synapse plays a short sound and listens for it, with your output left exactly as it is. The first run shows the macOS permission prompt.

      Step 3 of 5 · AI setup

      Connect your AI and transcription keys

      Step 4 of 5 · Your profile

      Add your resume and the job description

      Answers use only experience from your resume. Add both from the phone companion; you can do this any time before the interview.

      Step 5 of 5 · Test answer

      Send one test question

      This makes one small real request and shows how quickly the first words arrive.

      Ready

      You are set

      Press ⌥⇧T to start listening when the interview begins. The Session button in the title bar shows everything this setup checked, any time.

      Quit Synapse?

      Listening stops and the overlay closes.
